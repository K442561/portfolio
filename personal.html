A full-stack, mobile-first web application designed for students and working professionals facing strict deadlines and traffic uncertainty across all of India, with specialized local corridor modeling. Instead of only calculating the shortest distance or static GPS ETA, it calculates the probability of arriving before your deadline, determines the Latest Safe Departure (LSD) time with a live countdown timer, recommends a recovery plan if you miss a bus or connection, locks your chosen route with touch confirmation, and includes a learning loop that calibrates route estimates using real trip logs.
